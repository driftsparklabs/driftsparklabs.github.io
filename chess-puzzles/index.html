Chess Coach: Puzzles

100,000 chess puzzles, all on your phone

Chess Coach: Puzzles is a tactics trainer for Android. Every puzzle is a position from a real game, the puzzles you get match your rating, and the whole set is inside the app — no account, no connection needed.

Free · Offline · No account · No subscription

How it trains you

  • Puzzles at your level

    You answer one question when you start: how much chess have you played? After that your rating does the work — solve a hard one and it climbs, miss an easy one and it drops, and the puzzles follow.

  • Real positions, real lines

    Every puzzle comes from a played game. The opponent moves first, then you find the line that works — move by move, not one clever move and done.

  • Mistakes come back

    Miss a puzzle and it goes on your review list, so the tactic you did not see gets a second attempt later instead of disappearing.

  • A daily puzzle

    One puzzle a day, the same one for every player. Train on any day and your day streak grows; only skipping a whole day breaks it.

  • Fully offline & private

    All 100,000 puzzles ship inside the app. Your rating, stats and history stay on your device — there is no server of ours for them to go to.

  • No subscription, ever

    Free to play with kings as your training lives. They refill on their own, and a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited play and removes every ad.
