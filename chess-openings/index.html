Chess Coach: Openings

Learn the why behind every opening move

Chess Coach: Openings is a free, focused opening trainer for Android. Drill 11 classic openings move by move — as White or Black — with a hand-written explanation for every single move.

Free · Offline · No account · No subscription

Why this trainer is different

  • Learn the why

    Every move in every line is annotated by hand: what it does, what it prepares, what it prevents. Ideas stick — move sequences don't.

  • Drill, don't read

    Play the line move by move against a book opponent and get instant feedback. Wrong move? See what the book expects and why.

  • Both sides of the board

    Train every opening as White and as Black, with separate high scores for each side.

  • Main lines and sidelines

    Train the main line or branch into named variations — and switch lines mid-session when you find a move from another book line.

  • Fully offline & private

    No account, no sign-up, no tracking dashboard. Train on the plane, on the train, anywhere.

  • No subscription, ever

    Free to play with a daily refill. One single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited training and removes all ads forever.
