Driftspark Labs / Arrowwords

The arrowword from the puzzle magazine, on your phone

The clue sits in a cell of the grid and an arrow shows where the answer goes. 500 puzzles in American English and 500 in British English, plus 500 each in Dutch, German, Czech and Hungarian. Every set is written in its own language, not translated.

Free · Plays offline · No account · No subscription

Easy to play

  • Easy to read

    The clue for the selected word is shown large above the keyboard, so you never have to read the small cells. Zoom the grid if that reads better.

  • Three levels

    Easy, medium and hard, all the same size. The higher the level, the rarer the words and the harder the clues. Packs of 20 get a little harder each time.

  • A built-in keyboard

    No autocorrect getting in the way. It follows your phone's layout, such as QWERTY or AZERTY. Tap a cell twice to switch direction.

  • Help when you are stuck

    Checking is free: wrong letters turn red. Revealing a letter or a word costs hints, and your hints are topped up to 10 every day.

  • Challenge a friend

    After a puzzle, send the empty grid with your time. Your friend plays the same puzzle and sees the time to beat.

  • Play anywhere

    All puzzles are inside the app. No internet and no account needed. Unlimited hints are a one-time purchase, no subscription.
